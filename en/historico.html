How it is measured

  • Signal: an asset that enters the bullish or bearish list when it was on neither before.
  • Entry and exit: entry at the close of the session after the signal; exit at the close 5 sessions later, fixed in advance. For bearish signals the sign is flipped.
  • Versus the market: each signal is compared with the average of the same market on the same dates, so a general rise is not mistaken for skill.
  • Engine 2: "open" is whether the market opens above or below the previous close; "day close", whether it closes above or below. It counts as a hit if the average of the stocks moved in that direction.
  • Forward tracking: only signals since the record started count; nothing is ever computed with past data.

It does not include commissions or price differences when trading. With few closed signals the percentages mean nothing. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
