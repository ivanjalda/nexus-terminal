How it's measured

  • What a signal is: an asset that enters the bullish or bearish list when it wasn't on either before.
  • Entry: the close of the session after the signal, which is when you could actually act.
  • Exit: the close 5 sessions after entry. Always 5, fixed in advance.
  • Result: change in %. For bearish signals the sign is flipped (you gain if the price falls).
  • Forward tracking: only signals recorded since the system started are counted; nothing is computed with past data.

What this history does NOT include: commissions or price differences when trading. To avoid mistaking a general market rise for skill, each signal is compared with the average of the same market over the same dates. With few closed signals the percentages don't mean anything. Past performance does not guarantee future results and this is not financial advice.

Recorded signals are not deleted or modified: only their result is filled in once the 5-session period is over.
